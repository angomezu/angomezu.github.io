From AI Adoption to AI Success: Data and AI Governance as Predictors of Successful AI Implementation

Universidad Evangélica de El Salvador

A proposed empirical study of whether data and AI governance maturity helps explain why some organizational AI initiatives move into sustained use while others remain experiments. The research design includes a governance maturity framework and a quantitative study.

Proposal stage. No completed empirical results or publication are claimed.

2025Completed applied research

Plant Organ Segmentation from 3D LiDAR Point Clouds

East Tennessee State University · Oak Ridge National Laboratory collaboration

As part of a research team, I developed and evaluated a graph-based deep learning pipeline using PyTorch Geometric, local geometry descriptors and class-aware training. The task was to distinguish plant organs in point clouds affected by noise, occlusion and class imbalance.

Our best reported configuration reached 65.58% validation mean IoU and 82.27% stem recall under the project evaluation setup.

Completed project; the linked manuscript and code are not a peer-reviewed publication.

Research repositorySegmentation demonstration
