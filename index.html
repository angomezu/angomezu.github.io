2025Completed research project

East Tennessee State University
Oak Ridge National Laboratory collaboration

Plant Organ Segmentation from 3D LiDAR Point Clouds

Distinguishing plant structures in 3D sensor data requires a model to work with noisy observations and severe class imbalance. This ETSU team project, in collaboration with ORNL, studied geometry-aware learning for that problem.

Contribution and approach
As part of the team, developed and evaluated a Dynamic Edge Convolutional Neural Network in PyTorch Geometric. The methodology combined point-wise annotation, local geometric descriptors, class-aware loss functions, and bootstrap evaluation.
Evaluation
The strongest reported configuration achieved 65.58% mean IoU and approximately 82% stem recall under the project's evaluation setup. These are project-reported results.

Completed in 2025. The linked code documents an unpublished research manuscript, not a peer-reviewed publication.

From AI Adoption to AI Success: Data and AI Governance as Predictors of Successful AI Implementation

A proposed empirical study investigating whether governance maturity helps explain which organizational AI initiatives move beyond experimentation, remain operational, and create sustained value.
